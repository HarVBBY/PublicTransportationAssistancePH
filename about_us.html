PUBTA Features

Mission

Develop a system that will improve the effectiveness, accessibility, and passenger experience of jeepney transportation. This entails making routes more efficient, using technology for tracking in real time, and making sure that everyone can afford and enjoy the convenience.

Vision

PUBTA wants to lead the Philippines' commuting aid market by offering dependable, effective, and user-friendly services. It aims to make everyday trips smooth experiences by utilizing cutting-edge technology and a dedication to passenger convenience.
