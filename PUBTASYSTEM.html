      How PUBTA works

      1. Tell us where you're going. Type a landmark, school, mall, station, barangay or road (for example “UE”, “Monumento” or “Victory Mall”), use your current location, or pick a point on the map.
      2. Choose your options. Passenger type (students, seniors and PWD get discounts), when you leave, and which transport you're comfortable with.
      3. Press “Find routes”. You get the best options with total time and fare. Tap one to see every step: where to wait, which signboard to look for, where to get off and what each ride costs.

      Fares are the latest LTFRB/LRMC figures PUBTA knows about (updated Sept 2026); tricycle fares vary by city and are often negotiated. Times are estimates and consider rush hour.
